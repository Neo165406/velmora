Return & Exchange Policy

What to do if something isn't quite right.

Exchange Window

If you're not fully happy with your order, you can request an exchange within 3 days of delivery. Please keep the item unused, unworn, and in its original packaging with all tags attached.

Return & Refund

Returns are accepted only if the item received is damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered. Since orders are cash on delivery, please inspect your item at the time of delivery whenever possible. Report any issue within 24 hours of receiving your order by messaging us on WhatsApp with your order details and photos of the item.

Non-Returnable Items

Earrings, and any items marked under Combo Deals or Gift Items (flowers, chocolates, DIY kits), cannot be returned or exchanged once delivered, for hygiene and freshness reasons — unless the item arrives damaged or incorrect.

How to Request a Return or Exchange

  1. Message us on WhatsApp or email with your order details.
  2. Share clear photos/video of the item and its packaging.
  3. Once approved, send the item back — we'll confirm the pickup or drop-off details with you.
  4. Refunds (where applicable) or replacements are processed within 3–5 working days of receiving the returned item.

Questions about a return or exchange? We're happy to help.
